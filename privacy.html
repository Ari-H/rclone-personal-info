Privacy policy for rclone-personal

Effective date: November 16, 2026

rclone-personal is a personal Google OAuth client maintained by Ari-H. It is used only by its owner to access the owner's Google Drive with rclone on the owner's devices. It is not offered as a service to other users.

Google data access and use

Depending on the permissions granted, rclone accesses Google Drive file contents and metadata to list, download, upload, update, delete, mount, and synchronize the owner's files. Authorization is performed through Google. This informational website does not collect Google passwords.

Storage and retention

Rclone stores its OAuth credentials and tokens in its configuration on the owner's devices. Downloaded files, local synchronized copies, cached contents, and operational logs may also be stored on those devices. These remain until removed by the owner or the configured cache and retention settings. Files held in Google Drive remain subject to the owner's Google account settings and Google's retention practices.

Sharing

Rclone communicates with Google services to perform the owner's requested operations. This personal client does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or intentionally share it with unrelated third parties. Drive files, OAuth credentials, and tokens are not uploaded to this website or its public source repository.

This website

These static pages contain no application sign-in, analytics scripts, or tracking code. They are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Google services are subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting local data

The owner can revoke the client's authorization through Google Account connections and remove local configuration, cached files, synchronized copies, and logs from their devices. Revoking access does not automatically delete existing local copies or files in Google Drive.

Contact and changes

Non-sensitive questions can be raised in the project issue tracker. Issues are public: never include credentials, tokens, or private files. This policy will be updated if this personal client's use changes.
