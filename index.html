rclone-personal

rclone-personal is a personal Google OAuth client maintained by Ari-H for accessing the owner's Google Drive using rclone on the owner's devices. It is not a public application or an official Google product.

Purpose and access

The client authorizes rclone to list, download, upload, update, and delete files in the owner's Google Drive, according to the permissions granted during Google sign-in. This supports mounting and synchronizing files on the owner's devices. It is intended only for the owner's use.

This website is informational only. It has no sign-in form and does not receive Google authorization tokens or Drive files.

Privacy policy

For non-sensitive questions, use the project issue tracker. Issues are public: do not post credentials, tokens, or private files.
